# Warm Modern Meeting Room Brief

## Purpose

Create two independently designed, review-only meeting rooms for eight people.
The rooms are compared in the Vrata runtime under the same functional and
technical constraints. This pilot does not activate a product template.

## Shared Functional Contract

- nominal room envelope: 7.0 m x 5.0 m x 3.1 m, with at most 10% adjustment;
- one main entrance, one large window, and one presentation display;
- one `main` spawn with 0.5 m wall margin, 0.75 m open radius, and 2.0 m head clearance;
- exactly eight non-overlapping seat anchors;
- required media surfaces: `debug-main` and `whiteboard-wall`;
- safe routes from the entrance to every seat and the presenter/display zone;
- review at approximately 1.6 m eye height;
- four semantic views: entry, participant, presenter, and diagonal overview.

## Shared Visual Direction

- warm oak, light mineral plaster, and graphite metal;
- sand and muted grey-green textiles;
- daylight plus 2700-3000 K architectural and pendant lighting;
- credible window, door, ceiling, trim, furniture, hardware, HVAC, power,
  data, and cable-management construction;
- restrained lived-in details and moderate asymmetry;
- no neon, sci-fi language, gold decoration, branded products, mirrored
  symmetry, or furniture made from unexplained cubes and tubes.

## Independent Decisions

Each candidate independently owns architecture, layout, exterior treatment,
materials, lighting composition, furniture forms, props, and production method.
No accepted mesh, material recipe, or composition decision may cross between
candidates before freeze.

## Review Identity

Repository candidate numbers are not review labels. A coordinator assigns
`Alpha` and `Beta` randomly only after both art candidates are frozen. The
mapping stays unavailable to reviewers until scorecards are complete.
