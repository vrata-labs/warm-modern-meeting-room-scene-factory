# Warm Modern Meeting Room Style Sheet

Status: draft for the art-direction gate. No source image has been downloaded,
copied, or committed. Reference IDs resolve to metadata-only source-page links
in `reference-ledger.json` and are never model inputs.

## Composition

- Use one readable communal center with an asymmetric secondary storage or
  waiting edge.
- Keep entrance-to-seat and entrance-to-presenter routes obvious at eye level.
- Express the room envelope, window depth, door construction, and ceiling
  rhythm before adding decorative objects.
- References: `ref-architecture-01`, `ref-architecture-02`.

## Windows And Doors

- Build a deep window reveal with a graphite thermal frame, sill, gasket logic,
  and believable exterior depth.
- Use translucent or textured internal glazing only where privacy and borrowed
  daylight have a clear function.
- Use one flush warm-wood door leaf with graphite frame, hinges, handle, stops,
  and visible jamb depth.
- Reference: `ref-windows-doors-01`.

## Ceiling And Acoustics

- Keep a calm 0.6 m service rhythm and make lighting, ventilation, sprinklers,
  and acoustic treatment look coordinated.
- Prefer bounded textile or wood-wool absorption zones to random slats and
  hanging shapes.
- Do not mirror the whole ceiling or use arbitrary sculptural forms.
- References: `ref-ceiling-acoustics-01`, `ref-ceiling-acoustics-02`.

## Materials

- Primary palette: warm-medium oak, light mineral plaster, fine matte graphite
  metal, sand fabric, and muted grey-green fabric.
- Preserve material-specific roughness, edge radii, grain scale, fabric weave,
  and construction seams. Do not apply one bevel or roughness to every object.
- Keep metal accents functional. Gold, polished brass, and luxury-club styling
  are excluded.
- Reference: `ref-materials-01`.

## Furniture

- Design a credible eight-person table with visible structure, cable path,
  power access, edge build-up, and sufficient knee clearance.
- Chairs need plausible seat/back support, five-star or sled construction,
  real scale, and circulation clearances. Avoid branded product likeness.
- Match table shape and chair posture to conversation and presentation modes,
  not to a showroom composition.
- Reference: `ref-furniture-01`.

## Lighting

- Combine soft directional daylight with 2700-3000 K architectural and pendant
  lighting.
- Keep faces, the table surface, entrance route, and display wall readable
  without flattening the room.
- The first view must stay above average luminance 40 with dark pixels below
  70 percent under the agreed capture settings.
- References: `ref-lighting-01`, `ref-lighting-02`.

## Exterior

- Show real reveal depth, nearby ground or terrace, vegetation, and a restrained
  middle-distance context. Do not place a flat image immediately behind glass.
- Compare generic threshold and depth patterns; do not select a recognizable
  building or landscape composition to reproduce.
- Reference: `ref-exterior-01`.

## Lived-In Detail

- Add bounded, asymmetric groups: notebooks, drinking glasses, charging points,
  conference device, a few cables, books, and several plants.
- Every object must support meeting use, maintenance, power/data, or believable
  occupation. Decorative scatter without function is excluded.
- References: `ref-lived-detail-01`, `ref-lived-detail-02`.

## Rejected Directions

- signature glazed room with a rooftop garden;
- dark luxury-club palette with brass accents;
- residential concrete-arch composition and identifiable furniture;
- branded public-building geometry or organization identity;
- neon, sci-fi language, mirrored symmetry, gold decoration, random ceiling
  forms, and unexplained cube-and-tube furniture.

Approval of this sheet approves principles and measurable rules only. It does
not license source photography, approve any source as model input, or assign a
candidate repository to a production track.
