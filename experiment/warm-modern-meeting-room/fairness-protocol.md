# Fairness Protocol

1. Both candidates receive only the shared brief, approved style bible, and technical limits.
2. Final meshes, material recipes, composition decisions, and source files do not cross repositories before freeze.
3. Search, prompting, generation monitoring, failed attempts, cleanup, assembly, and reruns count as active production time.
4. One-time factory setup, queue time, GPU wall time, validation, and publication are reported separately and excluded identically for both tracks.
5. The equal active-human-time cap is set only after the AI probes and the comparable curated calibration.
6. Reaching the cap freezes a candidate. No extension is allowed after viewing the other candidate.
7. Candidate repository numbers remain neutral and do not encode production track or review label.
8. After both candidates freeze, a non-author coordinator randomly assigns `Alpha` and `Beta` and stores the mapping outside reviewer-visible repositories and diagnostics.
9. Review URLs, room labels, manifests, HUD, screenshots, and walkthroughs expose only `Alpha` or `Beta`.
10. Reviewers do not inspect repositories, provenance, diagnostics metadata, production time, or yield before submitting scorecards.
11. At least three non-author reviewers score both variants independently in randomized order. With one reviewer, results are qualitative only.
12. A difference below five normalized points is a visual tie. No subjective revote breaks a tie.
